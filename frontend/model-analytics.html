Error Analysis & Edge Cases

Real-world NLP models face nuances where queries share academic vocabulary across departments.

Observed Intent Overlaps:
  • exam_results vs exam_schedule: Both intents heavily share tokens like "examination", "semester", and "date". When a student asks "when will results be announced", TF-IDF features can occasionally overlap with exam schedules.
  • campus_amenities vs hostel_information: Questions about dining messes or on-campus shops share facility nouns.
  • admission_eligibility vs admissions: Queries regarding cut-offs and general application deadlines require fine-grained n-gram distinction.

Mitigation in UniAssist AI: Our hybrid architecture pairs the classifier with intent category boosting inside the FAQ retrieval engine. Even if intent score is borderline, the retrieval module verifies exact question phrasing to prevent incorrect answers.

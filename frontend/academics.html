Ordinance on Minimum Attendance Requirements

According to Section 4 of the University Academic Regulations, regular attendance in classes is an essential part of the educational process.

75% Mandatory Attendance Rule

Every student must secure minimum 75% attendance in theory lectures, tutorials, and practical laboratory sessions in each registered course.

Medical & Event Condonation:
  • Medical Grounds: In case of prolonged hospitalization or serious medical illness, relaxation up to 10% (minimum 65% aggregate) can be granted by the Dean on submission of authentic medical records within 7 days of returning to campus.
  • University Representation: Duty leave (OD) is granted to students officially deputed to represent the university in sports competitions, technical symposiums, or cultural festivals.
  • Debarment Consequence: Students with less than 65% attendance under any circumstances will be detained from appearing in End-Semester Examinations for that subject.

Tuition Payment Guidelines
  • Accepted Modes: Net Banking, UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or NEFT/RTGS bank challan via Student Portal.
  • Deadlines: Autumn Semester fees are due on July 31; Spring Semester fees are due on December 31.
  • Late Fees: A grace period of 7 days applies. Afterwards, Rs. 100/day late penalty is assessed.
  • Installments: Students experiencing economic hardship can submit an installment request to the Finance Registrar.
Merit Scholarships & Waivers
  • University Merit Scholarship: Top 5% students in each branch maintaining CGPA >= 8.5 with no backlogs receive up to 50% tuition waiver.
  • Merit-cum-Means Assistance: Available to students whose total family income is under Rs. 3,00,000 per annum.
  • National Scholarship Portal (NSP): All central and state government post-matric scholarships are processed through the Dean Student Welfare Cell.
